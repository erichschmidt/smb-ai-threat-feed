## AI risk of the week: Deepfake voice and video payment fraud

A deepfake is audio or video of a real person generated or cloned by AI, good enough to fool someone on a quick call or clip. At a small business this usually looks like an urgent voice note or video message from the owner or a vendor asking to wire money or change bank details, often outside normal hours. This week, write down one callback rule: any payment request or bank-detail change that arrives by voice, video, or message gets confirmed by calling the person back on a number you already had. Tell your bookkeeper, office manager, and anyone else who moves money that the rule exists, so the first verification step is not awkward to use.

## Jargon buster

- **Deepfake:** AI-made fake audio or video of a real person, used to impersonate them.
- **Voice cloning:** Recreating someone's speaking voice from a few seconds of their audio.
